No legal or ethical travel agency can guarantee a 100% visa grant, as final adjudication rests solely with the immigration authorities and consular officers of each respective embassy.

However, our visa team maintains an industry-leading 98%+ approval track record. We conduct rigorous pre-assessment of your documentation, verify bank statements, prepare verified hotel vouchers and flight itineraries, draft customized cover letters, and eliminate discrepancies before submission.

Processing time varies significantly by country and visa type:

  • Electronic Visas (eVisa): Azerbaijan (3–5 working days), Cambodia (3–5 working days), Bahrain (3–7 working days), Egypt (5–10 working days).
  • Schengen Visas (France, Germany, Italy, Switzerland): Typically 15 calendar days from your biometric appointment date at VFS/Gerry's.
  • Australia & Canada: Usually 4 to 8 weeks depending on embassy workload.

We advise submitting applications at least 4 to 6 weeks in advance of planned travel dates for sticker/embassy visas.
