HISTORICAL SANCTUARIES

Guided Sacred Heritage

Every package incorporates thorough historical excursions led by resident scholars who articulate the deep context of early Islamic history.

Sanctuaries of Makkah Al-Mukarramah

  • Jabal al-Noor & Cave of Hira: The mountain sanctuary of the first revelation of the Holy Quran.
  • Jabal Thawr: The historic cave of refuge during the Prophet's momentous Hijrah to Madinah.
  • Plains of Arafat & Jabal al-Rahmah: The sacred grounds of the Farewell Sermon and spiritual apex of Hajj.
  • Mina & Muzdalifah: The landmark encampments witnessing the rites of Ibrahim.
  • Jannat al-Mu'alla: The resting sanctuary of Sayyida Khadijah (R.A) and noble companions.

Sanctuaries of Madinah Al-Munawwarah

  • Al-Masjid an-Nabawi & Rawdah Sharif: Reverent salutations upon the Holy Prophet (PBUH) and prayer within the Garden of Paradise.
  • Masjid Quba: The foundational mosque of Islam, whose prayer holds the spiritual reward of an Umrah.
  • Mount Uhud & Shuhada Cemetery: Visiting the historic battlefield and the resting place of Sayyid al-Shuhada Hamza (R.A).
  • Masjid al-Qiblatayn: The historic sanctuary commemorating the divine redirection of prayer towards the Kaaba.
  • Saba Masajid: The defensive posts marking the triumph of Ghazwa al-Khandaq.
