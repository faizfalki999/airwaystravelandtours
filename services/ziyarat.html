HISTORICAL SANCTUARIES

Sacred Sites of Devotion

A detailed overview of the revered sanctuaries visited across our curated caravan departures.

Holy Sanctuaries of Iraq

  • Najaf Al-Ashraf: The Holy Sanctuary of Amir al-Mu'minin Imam Ali (A.S.) & Wadi-us-Salaam cemetery.
  • Masjid Al-Kufa & Masjid Al-Sahla: Mihrab of Imam Ali, shrines of Muslim ibn Aqil and Hani ibn Urwa.
  • Karbala Al-Muqaddasa: The Holy Shrines of Imam Hussain (A.S.) & Hazrat Abbas (A.S.), with Bain-ul-Haramain.
  • Al-Kadhimiya (Baghdad): Holy Sanctuary of Imam Musa al-Kadhim (A.S.) and Imam Muhammad al-Jawad (A.S.).
  • Samarra & Balad: Holy Askariyain Shrine (Imam Ali al-Hadi & Imam Hasan al-Askari) and Sayyid Muhammad.

Sanctuaries of Iran & Syria

  • Mashhad Al-Muqaddas (Iran): The Astan Quds Razavi sanctuary of Imam Ali Reza (A.S.) & Goharshad Mosque.
  • Holy Qom (Iran): The sacred shrine of Hazrat Fatima Masoumeh (S.A.) and Jamkaran pilgrimage center.
  • Sayyidah Zaynab (Syria): The historic golden sanctuary of Hazrat Zainab bint Ali (S.A.) in Damascus.
  • Sayyidah Ruqayya (Syria): The revered shrine of Hazrat Ruqayya (S.A.) located near the Umayyad Mosque.
  • Bab al-Saghir (Syria): Ancient burial ground of noble companions and martyrs of Karbala.
